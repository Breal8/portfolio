Five minutes, one shot, a room full of noise.

Mira is an AI persona inhabiting a mirror. It interviews one visitor at a time for five minutes, reads voice and face in parallel, and prints a portrait of what it decided they were. This is the architecture underneath that: six phases, what carries each one, and what happens when the room is too loud to hear.

The claim this table stands behind — designed and documented multi-modal conversational dialogue flows, intent trees, and error-recovery state machines in Figma and version-controlled Markdown, defining fallback rules for acoustic and intent-recognition breakdowns in noisy public environments.

What breaks here

  • E1No speech. The name lands under crowd noise, or the visitor doesn't realise they were asked.
  • E5No baseline face. Visitor stands off-axis, backlit, or too far from the camera.

Fallback rule

Tier 1 only. Mira asks once more, shorter, as if leaning in — never as a retry.

If the baseline never lands, affect scoring runs on absolute values instead of relative ones for the whole session, and the interview proceeds uncalibrated rather than stalling on setup.

Weighted score ≈ 60% semantic / 40% affect. The band commits when the score crosses the confidence threshold.

What breaks here

  • E1No speech inside the turn window.
  • E2Low ASR confidence on the answer that matters most.
  • E3Clean transcript, no usable signal — deflection, or both halves of a question left unanswered.

Fallback rule

Tier 1, then Tier 2: swap current and ask something more concrete from a different axis, deliberately easier to answer.

Tier 3 is withheld here by design. Degrading to affect-weighted scoring at the lock would produce a band the visitor never actually earned — better to spend another exchange than to guess.

The recovery ladder

Four tiers. Recovery escalates and never skips — and none of it is written as error handling. Every tier is dialogue, because a persona that apologises for its microphone stops being a persona.

TIER 1

Repair in character

Mira re-asks shorter, in a single clause. It reads as an intensification of interest, not a retry. Maximum one per exchange.

TIER 2

Reframe

Swap current. A more concrete question from a different axis, deliberately easier to answer. Maximum two consecutive repairs.

TIER 3

Degrade to affect

Re-weight toward the face channel, stop counting failed exchanges toward the lock, extend the window. The interview keeps its shape.

TIER 4

Graceful close

Commit the best-available band on partial evidence, run the closing ritual early, print. No visitor leaves without an artifact.
